## Taleem360, AR learning app (final year design project)
An augmented-reality learning app for Class 6–10 students of Pakistani government schools. It turns textbook diagrams into 3D models that students explore part by part, so they can see how each part works and understand the course visually. It won Best FYDP (Best Final Year Design Project) at Techfest 2026 at NED University. Built by the FYDP team with AR and 3D models. Ebad's role, from Semester 7 to the present: the subject tabs, the 3D model integration and ongoing development. The project is still in development and continues next semester.

## RFQ-to-Invoice SaaS for aviation parts trading
A multi-tenant SaaS that automates the RFQ-to-invoice workflow for aviation parts trading companies. It has launched and is now in pilot with early customers. Built with Next.js and Supabase, with a multi-tenant architecture. Ebad's role: solo developer. He designed, built and shipped it end to end.

## Ludo mobile game backend
The backend for a Ludo mobile game, built in Node.js with PostgreSQL and connected to the Unity game client. Ebad's role: backend development and the Unity-to-Node.js integration.

## Tiny Village
A single-player survival horror game made in Unreal Engine, inspired by Dead by Daylight. The player activates enemy-guarded generators across an abandoned village to unlock a hidden escape route. Team of 3. Ebad's role: the full game environment, the generator and gate logic, the UI systems, and a contribution to the combat system. The game was showcased at GameCon 2026 at NED University.

## Underwater VR Experience
An immersive VR ocean exploration experience deployed on Meta Quest 3, built in Unity VR. Players explore an underwater world from a submarine with full locomotion and gesture-based swimming controls. Team of 4. Ebad's role: design and implementation of the full ocean exterior environment.

## Two Lights One Escape
A 3D split-reality co-op puzzle platformer made in Unity 3D for two local players, who coordinate across mirrored rooms to escape together. Team of 3. Ebad's role: level design for 2 levels and environment art across the majority of all levels. The game won GameCon at NED University, taking 1st place.
