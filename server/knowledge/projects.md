## RFQ-to-Invoice SaaS for aviation parts trading
A multi-tenant SaaS that automates the RFQ-to-invoice workflow for aviation parts trading companies. It has launched and is now in pilot with early customers. Built with Next.js and Supabase, with a multi-tenant architecture. Ebad's role: solo developer. He designed, built and shipped it end to end.

## Ludo mobile game backend
The backend for a Ludo mobile game, built in Node.js with PostgreSQL and connected to the Unity game client. Ebad's role: backend development and the Unity-to-Node.js integration.

## Tiny Village
A single-player survival horror game made in Unreal Engine, inspired by Dead by Daylight. The player activates enemy-guarded generators across an abandoned village to unlock a hidden escape route. Team of 3. Ebad's role: the full game environment, the generator and gate logic, the UI systems, and a contribution to the combat system.

## Underwater VR Experience
An immersive VR ocean exploration experience deployed on Meta Quest 3, built in Unity VR. Players explore an underwater world from a submarine with full locomotion and gesture-based swimming controls. Team of 4. Ebad's role: design and implementation of the full ocean exterior environment.

## Two Lights One Escape
A 3D split-reality co-op puzzle platformer made in Unity 3D for two local players, who coordinate across mirrored rooms to escape together. Team of 3. Ebad's role: level design for 2 levels and environment art across the majority of all levels. The game won 1st place at GameCon.
